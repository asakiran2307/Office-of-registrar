1. Excel Dynamic Numbering Formula for Column B (Inward/Outward Sheets)
Copy this formula directly into Excel cell B2 to automatically look up codes from the Masters tab and format with 3-digit padded row sequence:
="JU/OOR/IN/" & VLOOKUP(E2, Masters!$A$2:$B$50, 2, FALSE) & "/" & VLOOKUP(F2, Masters!$D$2:$E$60, 2, FALSE) & "/26-27/" & TEXT(ROW()-1, "000")
2. Excel Data Validation Rule (Dropdown List) Formula
Apply to Department (Col E) or Document Type (Col F) for dynamic list validation:
=OFFSET(Masters!$A$2, 0, 0, COUNTA(Masters!$A:$A)-1, 1)
